# Quest Balance To-Do List

Use this list when planning or reviewing balance work. These are balance and
quality-assurance follow-ups; they do not block combat-engine implementation.

## General

- [ ] **Lessen WPD requirements:** Reduce excessively high WPD totals so quests remain enjoyable and realistically writable without using WPD to compensate for overly valuable rewards.
- [ ] **Limit quest-reduction stacking:** Allow no more than one Day-reduction effect and one WPD-reduction effect on the same quest.

## Major balance fixes

- [ ] **Quantitative Research:** Replace the saga-long +1 Ki and +5% HP benefit with a limited-duration or limited-battle reward.
- [ ] **Underwater Wreckage:** Increase its day cost, add a battle, or award a damaged spacecraft instead of a fully functional 2,000z ship.
- [ ] **The Old Red Ribbon Relay Station:** Increase its difficulty or make the Wayfinder consumable because the reward saves more days than the quest costs.
- [ ] **The Children of Paozu:** Replace the ambiguous +1d30 transformation reward with a clearly defined and smaller permanent bonus.
- [ ] **Protector of the Peace:** Clarify that the player rolls 3d100 and uses the highest result rather than adding the dice together.
- [ ] **Kryptonite:** Award one Meteorite Shard or limit players to using one shard per match.
- [ ] **Militarized Industrial Complex:** Add weapon-value limits and prohibit Legendary, Unique, custom, or previously fused weapons.
- [ ] **The Alpha Job:** Add prerequisites and a battle or replace the full Dragon Radar with a weaker prototype.
- [ ] **The Apple of Discord:** Prevent the item from forcing two uninvolved players into a battle without consent or administrator oversight.
- [ ] **Bitten:** Increase the quest cost or weaken Victorian Attire because the current reward is near-endgame armor.
- [ ] **Chunky Monkey:** Increase the quest cost, add a battle, or weaken the Capsule Corp Saiyan Battle Armor reward.

## Unequal reward choices

- [ ] **Smuggler’s Whiteout:** Strengthen the Gero Override Chip or weaken the Gero Evidence Drive.
- [ ] **The Papaya Grand Final:** Strengthen the Fixed Match Ledger so it competes with the permanent combat benefits of the Finalist’s Gi.
- [ ] **Tide of the Hollow Moon:** Strengthen the Alien Salvage Cache so it competes with the Marker’s permanent quest reductions.
- [ ] **The Red Brick Below:** Clarify exactly which rewards each route receives and strengthen the Prototype Android Core route if necessary.
- [ ] **The Open Sky:** Strengthen the Hijacked Power Registry because 300z once per saga is weaker than saving an entire quest day.
- [ ] **The Collector’s Cradle:** Strengthen the Ark Survivor’s Signal and Collector’s Core relative to the Hollow Moon Compass.
- [ ] **Paozu Heritage Day:** Strengthen the Forged Heritage Deed or slightly weaken the Trail Warden Badge.

## Under-rewarded quests

- [ ] **Rock Your Heart Out!:** Add EXP, increase the Zenni, or provide a small thematic item.
- [ ] **The Bright Blue Sea:** Increase the Zenni or add a reusable ocean-exploration reward.
- [ ] **Pirate’s Cove:** Add EXP or Zenni because Joint Mandatory participants receive no WPD discount.
- [ ] **The Siren’s Call:** Add an alternate reward for characters who do not have a halo.
- [ ] **Qualitative Research:** Add an alternate reward for characters without a compatible spacecraft.
- [ ] **Underwater Wreckage:** Add an alternate reward for characters who cannot pilot a Namekian Spacecraft.
