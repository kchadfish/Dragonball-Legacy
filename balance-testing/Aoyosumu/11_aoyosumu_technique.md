## Mechanical Audit

- All builds use exactly **8 SP** and respect the minimum 20% SP requirement in HP, Power, and Dexterity.
- At 8 SP, 4 Dexterity SP = **+3 Dexterity Bonus**, 3 Dexterity SP = **+1**, and 2 Dexterity SP = **-1**.
- Percent stat modifiers are treated as additive before application and final whole-number rounding.
- These are **TF1 checkpoint build snapshots**, not literal day-one sheets; the Mastery and prerequisite style techniques are assumed trained.
- No custom move is invented. Custom moves require staff balancing, so assigning one a fixed optimized value would not be reproducible.
- Exact canonical move wording remains in `reference/moves/aoyosumu.md`; this sheet preserves the optimized mechanical loadout and chassis.
