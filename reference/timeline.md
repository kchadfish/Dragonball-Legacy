## Dragonball Resurgence Timeline

## Age 785: Prince Vegeta IV enacted a plan to revive the Saiyan Race. He began by restoring Planet Vegeta with the Dragonballs.
## Age 786: Thousands of Universe 6 Saiyans migrate to Universe 7's New Vegeta to repopulate the Saiyan Race. Trunks and the Son family opt to stay on Earth.
## Age 798: Bulma and Bulla return to take over the Capsule Corporation upon the death of her father.
## Age 865: A pocket of Tuffle refugees are discovered and invited to return to New Vegeta to make up for their past violent history.
## Age 884: The last known Super Saiyan perishes in battle.
## Age 885: Due to frequent conflict between the universes travel between them has become strictly banned.
## Age 950: King Vegeta the IX is challenged to a duel by a teenager with the throne of the planet on the line. He loses and the Pinache dynasty begins.
## Age 967: Artificial Majin creatures begin to show up on Alpha Collective. All are pink and stretchy. After initial worries of an assault, the creatures show that they mean no harm and begin to integrate as a population on the planet.
## Age 985: Though far weaker than the androids of the past, technological discoveries cause them to be produced en masse on Earth.
## Age 1000:  A mysterious darkness descends upon Earth for three months. 
## Age 1000: Dragon Radars around the world stop working. 
## Age 1005: Prince Piper begins his five day reign of terror on Namek.
## Age 1024: Strange creatures begin to be seen throughout Earth but sightings are never able to be verified.
## Age 1033: Ryme consolidates the numerous space bandit groups into a new Galactic Army.
## Age 1050: The World Martial Arts tournament is restarted and Goro is crowned the first winner.
## Age 1078: Goro is defeated for the first in the 28th World Martial Arts tournament by a mysterious individual who forfeits the next match. 
## Age 1083: Ryme is defeated and exiled by his son Cheel.
## Age 1085: Dragon Radars begin working once more.
