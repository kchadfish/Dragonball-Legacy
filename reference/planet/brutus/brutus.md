# Brutus

Brutus' landscape is very similar to Earth, blue water, green fields, etc. However, unlike Earth, it is made up of two large islands connected by a man-made bridge. Brutus is technologically backwards, though consisting of some beautiful structures that are reminiscent of Earth's Roman and Greek Empires, including gigantic coliseums that are the prime source of entertainment.

Lacking any means of transportation other than one's own feet, the Brutii are naturally physically impressive specimens since they are expected to do hard manual labor from birth and walk long distances all the time.
