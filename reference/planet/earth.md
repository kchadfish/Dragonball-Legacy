# Earth

Earth is a very advanced planet of the North Galaxy, in large part due to the foundation of the Capsule Corporation. Flying vehicles, capsules that shrink inanimate objects down to pocket size, advanced robots, etc. inhabits the ‘blue marble’ planet. The architecture has since advanced as well, often employing spherical or ovoid sections held aloft by a cylindrical stem, though old world architecture still exists.

Earth consists of seven continents which were once broken down into countries but can now be considered a constitutional monarchy with a single King and one army that defends the planet from invaders
