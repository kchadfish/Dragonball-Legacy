# Alpha Collective

The Alpha Collective was once an entire collection of planets belonging to the Frieza Empire, but they were destroyed in the rebellion. The Changeling survivors gathered the resource-rich debris and created one of the greatest acts of terraforming the Universe has ever seen. Nearly a dozen immense asteroids, some the size of countries, have been hollowed out, kept together through gravity manipulators, and filled with cities that contain their own atmosphere.

Originally meant as a base for plotting revenge, the Changelings of old are all long gone and the Alpha Collective is now a trading post with a population in the tens of millions where traders, slavers, mercenaries, and other shady dealers meet for good business. The population is still largely Changeling, though they have amended their ways. That is Queen Snow's official stance, at least.
