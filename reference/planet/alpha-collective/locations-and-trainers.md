# Alpha Collective

## Locations

Sector 01
The busiest of all of the Alpha Collective's hollow asteroids, Sector 01 is famed as a docking yard and trading post for anyone seeking a good deal. Trade permits and tariffs are extremely low in this area which causes a large array of traders to show up no matter what their wares. To the outside observer, this state-sized asteroid also houses a rather large proportion of humans.
The truth is that these 'humans' are actually a large population of Tuffles, gathering connections and powerful allies while waiting to strike at their old enemies.
Dock 006
Dock 006 is famous as the ironically safest port in the Alpha Collective, considering it is used almost purely by pirates. This alcove is where pirates come to sell their wares or buy things with ill-gotten currency, and anyone who upsets that trust with clients and merchants tarnishes the entire piracy business. May the Kais have mercy on the upstart who thinks he's above this code, because the pirates will not.
Sector 02
When people think of the Alpha Collective, they're largely thinking of Sector 02. This sector has the highest crime rate out of any 'civilized' area in the Southern Galaxy. The reason for this is that the thieves and murderers who want to make a name for themselves or 'make it big' come here seeking prey, not realizing that half of the denizens are doing exactly the same thing.
To counter this, thousands of bodyguards offer their services in the area and it has become a frequent profession, though many are scam artists as well. If one needs an assassin or mercenary hired, this is the place to find the best. The only problem is surviving long enough to find them...
Big Cycle's
Descendant of a nearly dead Changeling clan, Cycle is a mercenary captain that has amassed the most power on Sector 02. If you absolutely need something done, and you have the money for it, you want Cycle's men.
Sector 03
The most guarded of all sectors, Sector 03 is the generator sector that supports the entirety of the Alpha Collective. This station is filled with well contained nuclear reactors and has mile long rows of complex technology used to process the gravity and atmospheric needs and shifts in the other sectors. Nobody has ever gotten in without Lord Frost's permission; at least, nobody willing to risk talking about it.
Sector 04
Sector 04 is both a vacation resort and a horrific place to be trapped. This hollowed asteroid is filled with an ocean containing all sea life found on the destroyed planets of the Frieza empire. Unfortunately, this means hundreds of immense predator species. The protected areas are amazing places to house a spa or cruise, but the six hundred miles of unprotected ocean are filled with squatters, small islands, monstrous sealife, and pirates.
Sector 05
This Sector is the prison for the rest of the Collective. An entire city-state of a prison, this hollowed asteroid is not as well linked to the others; escapees risk space exposure if they leave their cell for too long. The Changeling guards have no problem with this, but then again, neither do the Changeling prisoners.
The cells have KI-nullifying technology and the prisoners are treated harshly when the guards deem to deal with them, though many are simply ignored because there are so many cells to handle.
The Undersector
Rumors abound of an entire sector hidden underneath Sector 05, one that is run by an entirely different organization. The Changelings scoff at such an idea, but no official investigation has been done.
Sector 06 - 08
The similar looking sectors of 06-08 are all residential sectors for the Changeling race, each approximately the size of a small country with fine agriculture, gravity, and a dome roof that emulates a sun. While the Alpha Collective is largely considered a place for shady dealings and cutthroat mercantile, these three sectors were preserved for the Changeling race to exist without being bothered (too much) by visitors.
The cities within hold large white and red towers with banners hanging from the rooftops to the 2nd level to show which of the Changeling clans controls the building. The crime rate of these sectors is extremely low; any infraction at all leads to the Death Penalty for those without the proper connections. Generally, the closer you get to the Changeling Palace the more power the clans hold.
Sector 09
This sector is thought by many to be closed, but the truth is that the Tuffles have over a dozen cities hidden within the state-sized asteroid. It is here that the Tuffles used their cloning pods 200 years ago to replenish their population and prepare to strike back against the Saiyans. Oddly enough, no strike has been made.
The Tuffle population here often travels via gene-encoded teleport pads to Sector 01 and back, keeping a facade of a mild human population on the Alpha Collective while they make trade with those passing through.
