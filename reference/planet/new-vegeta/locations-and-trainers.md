# New Vegeta

## Locations

Vegeta
The capital city of the planet, Vegeta is home to the Saiyan monarchy, and because of this, foreigners are not kindly taken to here. Any non-Saiyan risks being either jumped or detained by the Saiyan Guard. It is a city famous for execution without trial: unless you have business with the Saiyan government or major businesses, it is best to avoid this city at all costs.
Controlled like a military compound, almost every large building has Saiyan Elites guarding it, with constant surveillance monitoring its streets.
Emperor Wrolen's Palace
Guarded by Wrolen's strongest soldiers, this large castle is home to the royal family, which claims to have the highest rate of Super Saiyans in the entire race. Many used to doubt the legitimacy of this claim as most royals are rarely seen outside of political gatherings, however, recent events proved beyond reproach that the mythic power of the Saiyan race is indeed strong in the Royal line.
Vaanz
Housing the planet's largest military complex, Vaanz is controversially viewed throughout the rest of the galaxy. Designed as one of the largest space ports in the entire universe, it is capable of deploying space pods anywhere in the galaxy within twenty-four hours. Built this way to allow Saiyan mercenaries to deploy strike-forces wherever necessary, many planets have sanctions against Vaanz. No one, however, has done anything about it in a militaristic sense.
Saiyan Space Port
Large tunnels, fitted with magnetic railings, they are capable of launching space pods at near-light speeds. Fitted with a multitude of cannons and missile launchers to ward off invaders, the Space Port is more than aware of Vaanz's sore reputation on the outside, and willing to defend its right to exist should it come to that.
Rein
While the long arm of the law on Vegeta is powerful, the monarchy's iron fist does not extend throughout the entire planet. In fact, the Emperor has taken a rather lax attitude to the goings on of the rest of the planet, so long as they do not threaten his legitimacy and cannot serve him in some way. As a result, the distant province of Rein outwardly serves as an example of a third world country on the Saiyan homeworld: plagued by disease and crime. This entire slum nation is merely a front, however.
A collective of Tuffles, now tens of thousands strong, have built an underground metropolis beneath Rein and have been using it to rebuild their strength. Rumors abound of the Tuffles rebuilding their strength in secret, but most Saiyans disregard this as a tale that parents tell to keep their children in line.
Grayrian Ocean
Despite possessing one of the largest oceans in the galaxy, Vegeta pays little attention to its waters. Quite capable of gaining both travel and clean water without its assistance, the Grayrian has gone largely unexplored and untouched. Grayrian houses a range of massive sea monsters and Saiyan pirates who often use their ships to conduct raids on coastal towns before going around the galaxy to sell their 'booty' on other planets.
Although a constant annoyance to the Emperor, the pirates of Vegeta are shrewd, skilled and possess more than enough power to rival the King's personal soldiers. As a result, the denizens of Vegeta have learned to live with them.
Pirate's Cove
A secret cave located in the very center of the Grayrian Ocean, Pirate's Cove is a miniature continent in and of itself: said to be home to an even more ruthless Saiyan culture. Murder and mayhem are the rule of law.
Akaru Jungle
A playful moniker for what the Saiyans were hoping to find, the Akaru Jungle is a massive desert, spanning most of the land. Raging sandstorms make it dangerous, so dangerous that bandits have not even tried setting up shop here. Mutated dinosaurs, feeding on each other and the mildly polluted oases roam the vastness; attacking any outsider that dare trespass on their territory in the never-ending pursuit of food.
Green Jungle
A terraforming project performed in the dead center of the Akaru Jungle. For 150 miles in any direction, this land is actually well forested with redwood trees and large vines of thick ivy, as well as many unique flora that grew from the changed soil and atmosphere. The Green Jungle did not turn out as the paradise that many hoped for, as endangered insects and massive dinosaurs took up residency with glee.
Wastelands
A wide expanse of caves, rocks and mountains, this is the territory where the Saiyans first landed on New Vegeta. Now merely a monument to their humble beginnings, the Wastelands now are home to escaped Saiyan criminals. Ripe with prostitutes, gambling, and murder, the Wastelands are where to go if you are in need of a contract killer but cannot afford the official Saiyan mercs.
