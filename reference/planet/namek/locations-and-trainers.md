# Namek

## Locations

Dai-bo Village
Located on the north-western continent, Dai-bo is home to the rare occurence of Fighter-type Namekians. Being dubbed a Fighter-type from birth means a Namekian has been born with an elite level fighting prowess. Few and far between, the Fighter-type of Dai-bo mix with the other castes who are slowly moving in and mixing the population. Fighter-types will usually grow to be part of the elite guard chosen to protect Guru, whose plateau can be seen from the coastline on an island not far off.
His modest throne room sits a top the plateau and a series of guards are always watchful.
Guru's Throne Room
A large oval-shaped building where the Guardian of Namek resides, he no longer possesses the same mystical strength his forefathers had: the secret teachings of Dragon Ball creation having been lost long ago, and the current set may be the last. Regardless, many still come to see the mighty Guru, in the hopes that he will deem them worthy and unlock their hidden potential.
Sahm Village
The village located on the south-western continent of Namek is home to the Warrior-type combat castes of the Namekian race. Being born a Warrior-type is considered a huge honor and gift as they are the second most gifted fighters from the planet Namek. In Sahm, while it is a friendly atmosphere, the children are competitive from an early age and maintain a proud tradition of practicing martial arts.
Ket-hon Village
The village located on the south-eastern continent of Namek is home to the Soldier-type combat castes of the Namekian race. These low-level fighters are born with superior combat abilities than most other races, but they are at the bottom rung of the combat caste ladder. Most do not take up combat professionally and instead raise children and becomes artisans.
Joko Cavern
The mountain range outside of Ket-hon village goes on for over 60 miles and has been carved into 4,000 ft. statues of warriors and heroes of the past. To be added to this mighty collection is the greatest honor that a Namekian can give to another, as the process takes over a decade with the assistance of nearly half the villagers.
Dahn-tu Village
The central continent on the Namek homeworld is home to Dahn-tu Village, which is broken up into a series of smaller villages throughout the large continent. Home to the majority of Healer-type Namekians, these Nameks are considered weak in combat but ultimately helpful in healing the wounded. Small, hospital-like buildings are scattered throughout the continent and warriors returning home from battles throughout the galaxy seek attention here from the magical hands of the Healer-types.
They are said to be able to painlessly heal almost any wound.
Dragon Clan Village
On a small island to the west of the continents sits Dragon Clan Village . . . or the remnants of the village. Members of the Dragon Clan were bestowed the great power and responsibility of creating the artifacts known as the Dragon Balls, though it is said none have been born in generations and that none will ever be born again. It is theorized that the Dragon Balls of Earth are actually tied to a rogue Dragon Clan Namekian who has not revealed himself, but it is only a theory.
The Village is mostly abandoned though their have been tales of raiders looking for clues to unlocking the real power of the Dragon Balls of lore, and rumors of sinister deeds going on in the quiet village.
