# Namek

Namek is the predecessor to a planet with a tragic history of destruction, an ironic fact given the mostly peaceful inhabitants. The planet is separated into small islands covered in blue grass and surrounded by green oceans. Namekian villages are numerous, but only a handful of ‘clans’ exist that these villages belong to. The Namekians are led by a Guru who's wisdom guides the races in both internal and intergalactic decisions.

Though often a peaceful planet, the Namekians have a strong warrior-caste and a population increase has led way to a stronger defensive force. Also to note is the planet’s two suns which keep the planet in constant daylight.
