# Basbas

The green rocky landscape of the planet does nothing to improve Basbas' beauty, nor does the rampaging hordes of wild horned dinosaur-like beasts that live on the outskirts of the walled in cities (which there are 3 of). The walled state of Basbas civilizations comes from their physical limitations, being a smaller race on a planet of otherwise enormous predators. Due to the limited real estate, the buildings have extended vertically.

Surprisingly technologically advanced, Basbas has hovercars and all the technology of Earth despite having no connection to the Capsule Corportion. It is also home to some of the most prestigious universities in the galaxies, though most of which specialize in fine-tuning the Bas-jins unique abilities which is of little service to most other races.
