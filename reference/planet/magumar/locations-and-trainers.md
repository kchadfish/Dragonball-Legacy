Planet overview (fallback)
The Maguma planet was blessed/cursed with lava running through it's 'veins'. However, it makes its presence known in the form of a singular lava pool (ocean) that surrounds the single island and scorches its beaches a good distance, which has turned the beaches for miles into a reddened semi-transparent grass. Further back from the beaches lie the cities, which has transportation that still requires wheels and basic robotics.
For the most part, Maguma is a normal planet with a robust musical and surprisingly 'surf' culture who do enjoy taking to the lava-filled ocean and riding the waves. Although not completely immune from the heat and the pain, they are naturally capable of rebuffing the permanent effects one would normally assume come from wading in lava, but with enough pain to warrant the actions as 'extreme'.
Hence, the youth have an immense influence on the culture of neighboring planets who wish to emulate their 'extreme' lifestyle ... often with less success.
