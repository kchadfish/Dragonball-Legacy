# New Vegeta

This planet was terraformed as the new home to the Saiyan race over 200 years ago. If color theory is to be believed, there may be something to the red sky that belongs to the planet and its correlation to the aggressive life forms that inhabit it, including the Saiyans and the Jurassic wildlife.

Originally a harsh desert wasteland, New Vegeta has been built up with advanced cities and terraformed farmland using "traditional Saiyan" designs, though they were actually stolen from the Tuffle race centuries ago. Vegeta is led by a royal line, where marriages are arranged between nobles and military officers of high rank or Power Level. Due to these trends, the original Caste system has returned and Third Class Saiyans are treated like labor slaves.
