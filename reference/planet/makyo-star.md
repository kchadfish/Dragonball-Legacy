# Makyo Star

The planet is red with swirling masses of blackness creating the likeness of a soul screaming in agony. The Makyo Star draws out the full dormant power of a Makyan when it is in the planet's presence, resulting in their power increasing by tens of times. The moving planet is currently in the Western Galaxy and moving on its way. There is very little light due to its nature as a roving planet that does not orbit a sun. Instead, its heat is generated internally through an unknown mechanism.
