# North Sector

## North City

North City is far less populous than the major cities of the other sections of the world but the inhabitants will get upset if you try to say they are less than other metropolis'. North City is renowned for its hunters who can navigate some of the coldest and harshest environments in the world. The city itself resembles a fortified military base more than a sprawling metropolis. 

## Jingle Village

A village that lies in the far north of the world, many of the residents of Jingle Village have never seen a land without snow on it. The village itself is small and relatively peaceful but it has the only known intact tower of the old Red Ribbon Army. The village has appropriated the structure and uses it as their town hall. The very top of the tower is still fitted with some of the detection technology of the old Red Ribbon Army, which has helped the village become known as the best scouts in the world.

## Yunzabit Heights
Yunzabit Heights - A land that was once said to have been where the old Kami of the world originated. Yunzabit Heights continues to be a land of mystery and is still not fully explored - even by the scouts of the North. Yunzabit Heights is a grassy area that looks to have been cracked by the hands of a god. A spiritual sect has frequent pilgrimages across the land to a sanctuary on the other side. 

## Northern Mountains

The Northern Mountains are filled with minerals and ore which has brought many looking to seek their fortune to the area. Unfortunately most large-scale efforts to mine the area have failed due to the frigid and inhospitable terrain. Rumor has it that the ruins of Dr.Gero's laboratory are somewhere in these mountains but they have never been found.
