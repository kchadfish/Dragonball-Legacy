# Earth

## Locations

North America
The pre-eminent continent of the Western hemisphere, this land is home to both the world's strongest combatants and greatest technologies. Often the source of Earth's most powerful industries, North America has recently become the richest continent on the planet due to the Android explosion: producing both the basic power absorption model, and later the infinite power core model. They recently were under a severe amount of skepticism by the rest of the world upon the introduction of the Bio-Android project. Regardless, with no other continent to reign them in, North America is piling ahead: intent on turning Earth into the undisputed most powerful planet in the galaxy.
Capsule Corp. Headquarters, West City
Founded by the late Dr. Briefs, Capsule Corporation is seen as a symbol of Earth's dedication to technological advancement. This is the main business branch of the company.
South America
Though not quite as rich or refined as their northern neighbors, South America produces some of the strongest martial artists in the world, possessing what some consider to be the finest Aoyosumu school in the entire universe. If there is a martial arts tournament to be had, you can be sure that a South American fighter will place either at the top or very close to it.
Recently however, due to the increased attention coupled with number of people interested in combat as a sport, most of the South American schools have sold themselves out: proliferating their art throughout the entire planet, even the galaxy. While Martial Arts is still their main export, other peoples are modifying and improving their teachings, making their continent slightly less valuable.
Korin Tower
Home to Korin, tiller of the Senzu Beans, the immortal cat still resides on the tower to this day. More than one thousand years old, he still gives beans to all fighters worthy enough to climb his tower and drink from his well. It is claimed he still possesses the ability to bestow Nimbus Clouds upon fighters, but this is more than likely a myth, but none-the-less it cannot be confirmed: no one has climbed his tower in more than one hundred years.
Kami's Lookout
This floating island of white stone is where the Guardian of Earth still resides. The current Guardian, a Namekian named Lyre, remains largely idle in Earth's going's on. With the majority of the Dragon Balls undiscovered by others for decades, it has been many, many years since he has called Z Fighters to the frontlines to defend Earth against evil.
Europe
The world's finest example of art and culture, Europe has almost completely forgotten about the militaristic tendencies still prevalent in other parts of the world. While still practicing Martial Arts as a form of exercise and focus, Europe's main focuses lie in intellectual pursuits: they produce Earth's greatest philosophers, inventors and (at times) politicians. Although occasionally their lack of militaristic capacity works against them--France has surrendered itself a record number of times to aliens who were landing there peacefully--Europe is still applauded as the greatest example of unity and tolerance in the entire universe.
Capsule Corp. Research & Development
While business operations take place in North America, the real science takes place in the R&D branch in Europe. Nothing goes to market without being thoroughly tested here first. Here is where they have perfected the art of breeding Saibamen through trade with Vegeta.
Asia
North America's singular rival for greatness, Asia's production capacity is unprecedented. Even while North America holds the patents for Androids, Asia is the primary producer of them, creating almost twice as many as North America does. While most continents were vying for North America to halt its production of Bio-Androids, Asia was busy trying to create its own. Failing only marginally, the bulk of Earth's military force comes from a combination of Asian and American soldiers. Despite this, a constant rivalry usually has the two continents working against one another, constantly trying to outperform the competition.
Satan City
Originally named Orange Star City, it was re-named for the warrior Hercule Satan who is said to have defeated the first Bio-Android and Majin creation single-handedly! Also home to Orange Star High School.
Kame House
On a remote island sits Kame House, a dilapidated building painted an odd pink color with a red roof. Barely readable on the side of the building is "Kame House" painted on in red. It was once home to the great teacher Kamesennin Roshi.
Red Ribbon Base
In the harsh cold terrain of Northern Russia, the Red Ribbon Army manages its military hidden amidst the mountains and snow.
Africa
Despite the fact that most continents have achieved some semblance of peace, Africa is still ripe with conflict. Internal struggles and a continuous stream of military coups have ensured that no form of stable government can possibly exist. This, in turn, has resulted in an unprecedented group of people living below the poverty line. With an average life expectancy still barely exceeding thirty, Africa contributes a degree of chaos and war to Earth that is unseen on most other planets. Making the planet Earth one of the most diverse planets, in terms of socio-economics, in the entire galaxy.
Diablo Desert
Home to a mighty group of bandits with a leader proclaiming himself Yamcha XXIV, Diablo Desert is mostly a forgotten part of Earth's geography. With the ruins of a castle still within its borders, it is largely avoided by people so as not to call up any trouble from the notorious thief.
Australia
After being converted to the world's foremost food supplier via various technologies, Australia is now no longer inhabited by humans. Run exclusively by machines, the natural growth of fruits and vegetables has created an eco-system unlike any other on earth. Housing a variety of insects and animals (including dinosaurs), Australia is often used as an example of Earth's diversity -- a bastion of beauty and nature.
World Martial Arts Tournament
The Tenkaichi Budokai Tournament takes place every three years at this location. It has hosted some of the world's most fiercest competition.
