# East Sector

## East City

East City is home to a large and vocal xenophobic movement. Long, long ago the entire city was decimated by a pair of aliens and many in the city have not forgotten the damage caused - though others are much more accepting and push back against the xenophobia.

## Mount Paozu

Mount Paozu is the largest habitat of the dinosaurs on Earth. The mountains themselves are vicious and difficult to traverse but if one travels they can find two places that are miraculously well preserved for an unpopulated area. The first is what appears to be a normal house. The other is a small wooden shack high in the mountains. While both show their age, there has been a clear attempt to fix the buildings and keep them in good shape.

## Satan City

North of Mount Paozu but south of East City, Satan City has grown tremendously as residents move out of the hateful East City. It's population is disproportionately filled with the "outcasts" of the world who don't look like humans. The city was renamed after the legendary Mr.Satan when he saved the world from the villainous Cell. The city still celebrates his victory as Satan Day where they have a parade with large balloons - mostly featuring Cell and Mr. Satan.

## Forests

Large forests sprawl the area between major landmarks in the east. Hidden in these forests are various ruins and wild animals. Hikers are found of looking for abandoned roads to follow to find the sites of untold history.
