# Earth

Earth is a very advanced planet of the North Galaxy, in large part due to the foundation of the Capsule Corporation. Flying vehicles, capsules that shrink inanimate objects down to pocket size, advanced robots, etc. inhabits the ‘blue marble’ planet. The architecture has since advanced as well, often employing spherical or ovoid sections held aloft by a cylindrical stem, though old world architecture still exists.

Earth has been ruled by a monarchy for generations. The government has split the world into four separate sectors based on their position in the world - North, South, East, and West. Each of the cities has a Governor who handles much of the day to day governing of each of the sectors with the King overseeing the larger operations of the planet. The capital of Earth is the Central City - which doesn't fall within the authority of any of the governors.

Kami's Lookout
This floating island of white stone is where the Guardian of Earth is said to reside. If a guardian still exists, they have not been seen in a century. Largely regarded as a myth, the dragonballs which were once said to have existed throughout the world thanks to the guardian have not been seen in a hundred years.
