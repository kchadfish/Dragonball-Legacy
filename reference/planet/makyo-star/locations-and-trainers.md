# Makyo Star

## Locations

Atra Tempestates
Three dark storms exist on the Makyo Star. They are large enough that they pierce through its red atmosphere and can be seen from space quite easily. They have raged on through all eternity and legends say that they will continue to rage for they are the wrath of the Makyan God manifested and the god's wrath can never be sated.
Left Eye
The "Left Eye" of the storm. This storm is the smallest of the three. Because of this, it has become a prime spot for Makyans to come and worship. They withstand their god's fury and attain some measure of enlightenment. Of course, they choose this spot because it generally doesn't kill people - at least not immediately. They have even chosen to build a grand temple here.
Right Eye
The "Right Eye" of the storm. This storm is the middle of the three and forms the right eye of the "face" of the planet. In the center of this storm, there are the ruins of a black castle. No one knows how it has survived in the center of the storm or who built it. Not even the toughest of the Makyans can reach the center of the storm without protective gear.
Mouth
The "Mouth" of the storm. This is the largest storm on the planet and no one ever visits it. Entering this storm is suicide, even with protective gear. No one has seen its center but it is said that a powerful demon, perhaps the Makyan God himself, lives there. Of course, the naysayers say that the planet itself is God and thus God cannot live in the center of the storm.
Garlic Castle
Garlic Jr.'s residence. The massive castle is an almost identical recreation of his old residence on Earth. The castle is so massive that it pierces the clouds, which provides one an excellent view of the red Makyan sky. It sits on the opposite side of the planet as the storms. Garlic Jr. rules the planet; however, the religious fanatics near the storms tend to annoy Garlic Jr. with their incessant preaching.
Fields of Blood
A field where the grass grows in a dark crimson color. Not only the grass, but all plants show this color in some way, shape, or form. Flowers have petals with this color and trees have leaves of the color. None have been able to identify the strange mutation in the plants of the region but it has become both revered and feared due to the mutation.
Many denizens believe that stepping on the grass in the Field of Blood or destroying a plant in the field can cause one to have bad luck for the rest of their life.
Mararyl's Cabin
One of the only buildings in the Field of Blood. It is a log cabin, made with normal logs and not the kind found in the Field of Blood. Mararyl is a small blue woman wearing a black hat with a strange blade on top. She is feared and hated by the populace for her arrogance and how she wantonly steps on and picks the flowers of the field.
