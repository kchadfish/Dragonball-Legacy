# Taifuu

## Locations

Fossun Villages
Filled to the brim with fishermen, cruise boats, and tropical-themed bars, the four dozen Fossun Villages mark the archipelagos of western Taifuu and are a peak attraction for tourists. The outlying areas surrounding Fossun Villages are often filled with rejected workers who turn to crime, or even skilled criminals seeking cover to mask their arrival.
Aturtai
The Taifuu-jin one would expect from the Alpha Collective or former Ginyu Force likely grew up in Aturtai. A space port city and military training ground, this floating island-fortress of steel and hydroponics hosts extendable bridges that allow it to connect islands from miles away, though they are more commonly used by Taifuu-jin as racing platforms.
Yuzon
A lush tropical jungle spanning across hundreds of miles, Yuzon is home to a plethora of Taifuu tribes and dangerous wildlife. Most notably, the jungle is home to special plants renowned for their curative powers. These plants have been nearly hunted to extinction by New Zealand poachers who seek them as a viagra replacement.
Goldrush
Seekers of fortune and fame will inevitably find themselves at the Goldrush, Taifuu's hottest casino. Shaped like a yellow tornado and spanning 64 stories in height, the massive Goldrush building has been the talk of the Galaxy over and with good reason. On top of the high profile gambling going on, the Goldrush Casino is the greatest attraction for talent agents in the entire West Galaxy.
Basil Bayou
Not all Taifuu-jin are kind of foreigners. Taifuu is home to a swampy region known as the Basil Bayou, filled with immense reptiles and thousands of water-running Taifuu-jin who do not take kindly to strangers and speak in strange tongues. Many Humans have attempted to speak with the denizens of the Bayou on the grounds that their planet hosts a similar region and culture; none have reported how the Taifuu-jin reacted, because none have returned.
Everstorms
The poles of Taifuu have the same temperature as the equator, with the exception of windchill. This is an important detail, as the poles are in a permanent state of hurricane. Often the source of major storms travelling the planet, the Everstorms are known as a 'Galactic Let Down'. After centuries of mystery, several explorations finally succeeded in discovering what lies at the eye of each pole's constant wind vortex. Their discovery? More wind.
