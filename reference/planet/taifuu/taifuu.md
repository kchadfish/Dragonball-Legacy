# Taifuu

Taifuu is the polar opposite of Magumar, which happens to be in the same solar system. The climate on Taifuu, to humans, has been described as perfect. A tropical paradise filled with beautiful trees and plantlife and beachside resorts. The technology and architecture of Taifuu is on par with its Maguma neighbors as they often trade with each other.

The planet would be a nearly perfect utopia if it wasn't for its terrible storm season which constantly threatens the coast and shuts down tourism for 3-4 months out of the year.
