# Shi’kir

Shi'kir was once filled with wild grasslands, but now all that is left of its beautiful landscape is the old mountain ranges that are now used to military testing and drills. The entire world is in a constant state of military preparation as the inhabitants are known throughout the galaxy for their leadership and combat potential.

A few cities are sectioned off and known as military-free zones for visitors complete with marketplaces and all the amenities of a normal tourist spot, but by-and-large, the Shikirian military owns the planet and every Shikirian is required to join at the age of 16. While not intentional, casualties are a fairly common occurrence during their war drills, and occasionally a small civil war will have to be squashed when one Commander's ego falls out of line.
