# Shi'kir

## Locations

District 1
This commerce District hosts the most ports and businesses of Shi'kir, often in rivalry with District 5 for their willingness to sell off-world tech. The cities of District 1 also host the most hotels and entertainment studios, with the exception of Elsiria which has banned all media outlets and allied with District 3 after purging the Red Light Cartel from its borders.
Districts 2 & 6
Both Districts 2 and 6 are used as 'safe zones', exempt from most wars at the risk of bringing the wrath of neutral Districts and even offworld Shikirians into the fray. This is because both these Districts are not only retirement rewards for all aged soldiers, but also breeding grounds. Millennia of adaptation to war has granted the Shikirians a second healthy fertility cycle that kicks in at the same time that their bodies begin to weaken from age.
All those who threaten the 'promised land', the Shikirian decade of partying after a life of war, are slaughtered with extreme prejudice.
District 3
Run by the Horned Legion, District 3 is the longest standing District Government. It shows. Brutal in their training and treatment of weakness, fond of gladiator fights and keen on conquest over agriculture, the Shikirians of District 3 often set a name for the race as a whole. Ruled by Emperor Robustus Caesar, the Horned Legion practices war games of its own where the losing sub-clans are forced into farming, fishing, and construction for the sub-clans who subjugate them.
The Emperor's council is filled with leaders of the strongest sub-clans, with failed coups occurring every few years.
District 4
Once a center of mercenary organizations following the guidelines of 'survival of the fittest', District 4 is now a nuclear wasteland with green clouds of radiation covering hundreds of miles and only contained by energy walls erected by District 3 and 8 after a long and bloody war ending mere months ago. Several teams have been sent into the wasteland to report on its status and search for lost resources, but none have returned without casualties from the hideous creatures who stalk its irradiated ruins.
District 5
The technological gem of Shi'kir, District 5 hosts the primary R&D and defense contracts that the planet scores with outside governments. District 5 has a heavy ban of all off-world technology unless it has been licensed for reverse-engineering, and thus has a large police state to enforce constant confiscations. The PTO has been banned from this district of white skyscrapers and industrial sized domes.
District 7
District 7 has been attempting registration as a safe zone, which puts it at odds with District 6 since there can be only two. Filled with rich water underneath its rocky surface, District 7 is home to many power planets which paint it as a target during civil war scenarios. Much to its chagrin, the fallout from District 4's destruction has lowered its odds of ever being a true Safe Zone by causing mutations among some of its outlying cities.
Monster Hunters have traveled the Galaxy for contracts against these frightening and mad Shikirians.
District 8
Central command for all interplanetary action on Shi'kir is held by the government of District 8. This urban wasteland trains members of other Districts in exchange for resources and fealty, though this has not stopped attempted takeovers every time. District 8 is famed most for the destruction of District 4, using nuclear technology to aid District 3's war and utterly oblitering an 8th of the planet in a surprise attack as vengeance for previous aggression.
