# Basbas

## Locations

Chronos University
The most famous University on Basbas. This University welcomes students of all races, as many come from across the different Galaxies to attend this University. It does have a degree entirely devoted to Time Manipulation. If you are accepted at this University, be prepared to be recognized throughout the Galaxy.
The Dimensional Rift
The Time Manipulation of Basbas has begun to get out of hand. A giant rift has opened in the sky, looking like a giant deep red wormhole. Beings from all across the Multiverse have begun streaming through it.
Skuld
Skuld is one of the largest cities in the Universe. No one in Skuld owns their own house. They all live in apartments that are as tall as the average skyscraper on Earth. They settled here because the energies which allow them to manipulate time are at the strongest here. This city is where Chronos University exists, as well as the largest research institutions on the planet.
Some say that if you get lost in Skuld then you will never be able to find your way back due to its cramped and confusing layout of interconnecting roads and walkways.
Veliuona Forest
The Forest of the Goddess of Eternity. This forest stretches for thousands of miles in either direction and is famous for its inability to be mapped. Every time you travel through it, it will look completely different due to the constantly shifting nature of time here. Some days it will be a large prehistoric forest with beasts beyond description and at other times it will be an empty field full of flowers. It doesn't all change at once, either.
It is not uncommon for travelers to stumble upon patches of another time which seem so out of place that some question whether they are even from this dimension.
Gorgon-Ru
There is a duality to all things in the universe, and this is especially true on Basbas. While Time flows strangely and unexpectedly elsewhere, the great swamp of Gorgon-Ru seems frozen. The marshland knows no daylight, remaining in a permanent state of twilight. The most disturbing facet of this timeless mire is found below the surface. Thousands of statues rest in the many pools of murky water, all posed as if attempting to reach for the surface or claw their way out.
Beware all who enter, lest you discover firsthand what created this haunting display.
Lumar Bok
Near the edge of the swamplands and the forest, but far enough away from the metropolis, Lumar Bok is the quiet little town of xenophobic green dwarves you always dreamed of... weirdo. What they lack in hospitality they more than make up for in talent, as some of the greatest time manipulators seem to migrate here to enjoy the quiet life. If it is training you want, this is the best and worst place to look.
