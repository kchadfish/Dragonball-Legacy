Haokiru

We shall heal our wounds, collect our dead and continue fighting.
- Mao Tse-Tung

There is one thing all fighters must have in order to fight: the body. Preserve the body, and in turn, you preserve the means for victory. If you can't stand, you can't fight. Use cautiousness in battle to prevent your opponent from capitalizing on a mistake, but also remain constantly ready and able to capitalize on your opponent's mistakes.
