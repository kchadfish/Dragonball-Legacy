


Midorikatai

If you kill enough of them, they stop fighting.
- Curtis Lemay

A fighter's skill and cunning mean nothing in a real battle, as these things are easily disrupted in the spontaneous events of combat. All things must be done from a position of power, or not at all. Brute force allows a fighter the ability to be flexible and adapt to combat situations. Eliminate the element of surprise, and the battle is left to your opponent and yourself in a test of might.
