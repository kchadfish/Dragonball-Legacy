Aoyusumu

Our capacity to retaliate must be, and is, massive in order to deter all forms of aggression.
- John Foster Dulles

A fighter's power comes from the mind, and a fighter must be able to focus the body and mind in unison, and dull all crude emotions to succeed in battle. There is little a fighter can do to win a battle but to ensure that he does not lose it, and to wait for the opponent to present his own means for defeat. Evasive and defensive tactics will allow you time to gain an upper hand in battle. If you can't get hit, you can't get hurt.
