Akaikaru
Victory is always possible for the person who refuses to stop fighting.
- Napoleon Hill

Continuity is strength, and hesitation is weakness. Momentum is the source of power, and to pause even for a second is to forfeit that power. There is no need to defend if you cannot be struck, so do not allow your opponent the opportunity to mount an unanswered offensive.
