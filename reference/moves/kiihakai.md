Kiihakai

Destruction, hence, like creation, is one of Nature's mandates.
- Marquis de Sade

There is a force far greater than that which can be seen. A fighter's true abilities come from the inner Chi, a force that can bring forth destruction so great, nothing can replicate it. A fighter's Chi controls movement, and a fighter must be able to harness this energy to fend in a battle. This energy takes many forms, and various purposes, but it is ultimately a destructive force.
