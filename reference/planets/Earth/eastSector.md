East City
___________________-

East City is one of the most inhospitable cities on planet Earth. Long, long ago the entire city was decimated by a pair of aliens. To this day there is a culture of prejudice and hate towards any creatures or people that don't look like normal humans.

Mount Paozu
___________________

Mount Paozu is the primary home of the dinosaurs on Earth. The mountains themselves are vicious and difficult to traverse but if one travels they can find two places that are miraculously well preserved for an unpopulated area. The first is what appears to be a normal house. The other is a small wooden shack high in the mountains. While both show their age, there has been a clear attempt to fix the buildings and keep them in good shape.

Satan City
______________________

North of Mount Paozu but south of East City, Satan City has grown tremendously as residents move out of the hateful East City. It's population is disproportionately filled with the "outcasts" of the world who don't look like humans. The city was renamed after the legendary Mr.Satan when he saved the world from the villainous Cell. The city still celebrates his victory as Satan Day where they have a parade with large balloons - mostly featuring Cell and Mr. Satan.

Forests
___________________________

Large forests sprawl the area between major landmarks in the east. Hidden in these forests are various ruins and wild animals.
