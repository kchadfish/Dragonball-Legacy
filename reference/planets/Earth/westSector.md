West City
_________________________

Home of the Capsule Corporation. West City is the technological hub of Earth. The city features numerous bridges and circular shaped buildings that aren't found anywhere else on Earth. Some attribute this to the alien heritage of the Capsule Corporation's CEOs.

Capsule Corporation: The Capsule Corporation has grown into a sprawling facility split into two. One side features a high-tech laboratory where the latest technology is developed while the other is a factory for manufacturing the various items they sell. Security at the Capsule Corporation is so great that it is said that no one has ever managed to break into their laboratories.

Dream Land - A world renowned amusement park that sits atop a large tower. Guests must take an elevator to the top of the tower to ride the numerous rides present. This isn't for the feint of heart, but don't worry about falling off the tower. Dream Land's top is surrounded by a dome which makes it perfect for fun all year round!

West City College - The most prestigious university in the world has come upon troubles recently with rumors of rogue robots causing mayhem throughout the campus at night. If you don't mind the threat of the robots then you may benefit from visiting Dr.Lacey - the famous professor and sister of Capsule Corporation's CEO. 

The Forests
_______________________

Ruined Towers - A few ruined towers are scattered throughout the woods surrounding West City. These enigmatic buildings are all made of red brick. Rumor is that an old military group had built them but they fell into disrepair long ago.

Sacred Land of Korin -  In the middle of the forest lies a village with a giant tower in the middle of it. The village is generally peaceful to outsiders - unless you wish to disrupt the forest or the tower.
