Papaya Island
__________________________________

Papaya Island has a few villages but is mostly known as the site of the World Tournament where the strongest fighters in the world come to prove their strength. 

Southern Seas
____________________________
The seas in the southern portion of the world attract many tourists due to their renowned beauty. The water is almost crystalline in appearance and the ocean life is often pleasant and willing to let humans get close to them. Each island within the seas has its own atmosphere and culture. Some are populated while others are remote and inhospitable.

On one of the smallest islands in the sea there is an old house with a martial arts master. Master Kameko is elderly and more than a bit of a curmudgeon but her tutelage is said to be some of the best in the world - if you can convince her to take you on as a student. She only has one student currently and hasn't taken on another in years.

There is a darker side to the southern seas that most don't see, though. Some islands have hidden coves where pirates lurk to prey upon unsuspecting travelers.

Diablo Desert
________________________

The Diablo Desert is the largest desert in the world. If you can stand the extreme heat then you can find numerous areas worth investigating within the desert - including the ruins of a castle once said to belong to Emperor Pilaf and the newly relit Fire Mountain.

Fortuneteller Baba's Palace
__________________________

Far to the west in this sector is Fortuneteller Baba's Palace. An old and eccentric lady is said to be able to see the future in her crystal ball at a surprisingly well built palace. Baba's palace consists of four buildings: the front entrance, the hall, the fighting arena, and the Devil's Toilet. At the main entrance there is always a line of people waiting for their fortune to be told.


South City
_________________________

South City is the main economic hub of this sector of the world. Due to the numerous tourist attractions in the south, South City has become known as a tourist hub where you can take busses to the coast for cruise ships. Lately there has been an effort to create a tour bus that goes through Diablo Desert but it has been unsuccessful so far.
