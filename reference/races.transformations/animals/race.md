Skin Color: Varies depending on species 
Appearance: Any animal species from Earth, sometimes anthropomorphic 
Planet of Origin: Earth
Biography: While 'Animals' is certainly a generic term for a range of species, in this instance it refers to the unique Animals of Earth who have taken anthropomorphic qualities. Capable of speech and bi-pedal movement in some cases, these Animals live amongst the Humans in their daily lives, with all the sentience of their Earth neighbors. For some unknown reason, Animals have an inherent ability to shapeshift that goes ignored and undoable by some through sheer lack of practice, but many have taken classes with the infamous Shapeshifting School to learn to temper this ability. With this ability they are able to transform their appearance into that of nearly anything depending on their skill level, including other races.
