Makaioshin Transformations

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 1 — Demonic Will[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: A blood-red flame aura channels Demon Realm power.
Stats: +30% Power, +10% HP, +10% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Successful single-die attack roll 30+ on base 1d32 or lower STUNs.
[INTERMEDIATE] Same; when opponent rolls natural 1 to attack you, pay 1 Ki to STUN them.
[MASTERED] Primary threshold becomes 29+; natural-1 retaliation remains.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 2 — Soulless[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Shadowy aura and blackened carapace; details vary but eyes glow red.
Stats: +60% Power, +20% HP, +20% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Successful 29+ (base 1d32 or lower) STUNs; a 27 may spend 3 Ki to STUN; opponent natural 1 may be answered with 1 Ki STUN.
[INTERMEDIATE] Primary threshold 28+; 27 may spend 3 Ki; natural-1 response remains.
[MASTERED] Primary threshold 28+; 27 may spend 2 Ki; opponent natural 1 automatically STUNs.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 3 — Lord of Darkness[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Demonic power intensifies into a lordly underworld form.
Stats: +70% Power, +55% HP, +25% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Successful 28+ STUNs; 26-27 may spend 2 Ki; opponent natural 1 auto-STUNs.
[INTERMEDIATE] 26-27 STUN cost drops to 1 Ki.
[MASTERED] As Intermediate; when this transformation effect STUNs, roll 30+ to gain 1 Ki.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 4 — King/Queen of the Underworld[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: A peak demonic-divine form representing rulership of the underworld.
Stats: +115% Power, +55% HP, +30% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Successful 28+ STUNs; 26-27 may spend 1 Ki; natural-1 attacks auto-STUN; after a transformation-effect STUN, roll 29+ to gain 1 Ki.
[INTERMEDIATE] Ki-gain check improves to 28+.
[MASTERED] Ki-gain check remains 28+ and STUNs caused by this transformation cannot be negated.
