[color=#ee4a2d]RACIAL TRAITS[/color]
[hr]
[color=#ee4a2d]Demonic Potential[/color] - At match start roll 1d10; 5+ allows starting at 10 Ki. Opponents cannot reduce starting Ki. If delayed, gain 5 Ki when it activates.
[color=#ee4a2d]Petrifying Spit[/color] - RESTRICTEDx1 after a successful single-die attack: pay 1 Ki to STUN. If you attack next turn, the opponent cannot BLOCK and suffers -5 defensive result.

[color=#ee4a2d]Classes[/color]
[hr]
[color=#ee4a2d]Aspiring King[/color] - New transformations start Mastered at 1d100; Ki gains cannot be prevented or reduced.
[color=#ee4a2d]Magic Materializer[/color] - Start with +1 Weapon. Weapon attacks +2 dice sides; Weapon-required Skills cost -1 Ki, minimum 1.
[color=#ee4a2d]Meditative Evil[/color] - Demonic Potential succeeds on 3+. RESTRICTEDx1 at 0 Ki: gain 2 Ki without using the turn.
[color=#ee4a2d]Feared By The Gods[/color] - Opponent transformation rolls fail on 10 or less, even with 1d100. RESTRICTEDx1: revert opponent one transformation level without using your turn.
[color=#ee4a2d]The Baddest Seed of the Kaiju Tree[/color] - Kills outside quest battles grant +25% EXP, or +50% if opponent had higher PL. Attack rolls costing 2+ Ki gain +1 result.
