Generic Classes
[color=#fff]These are classes that can be taken by any race.
[list]
[*]Weaponmaster - You may start the game with +1 Weapon item in your Inventory. Your attacks that require a Weapon gain +2 dice sides. Your Skills that require a Weapon cost -1 KI Point to a minimum of 1 to use or activate. 

[*]Dragon Ball Hunter - It takes -1 Day for you to find a Dragon Ball. Dragon Balls take 0 item slots for you. When you would lose your Dragon Balls due to death or escape, you may choose as many as you wish to be scattered instead. When you have a Dragon Ball in your possession, your attack rolls gain +1 dice side.

[*]Military Mentality - You start the game with +3 Training Days.

[*]Bodyguard - At the start of each week, you may choose another Player in the game. You are considered their Bodyguard for the week and may fight their Battles in place of them provided you are on the same planet. You do not have to roll an interference roll during that player’s battles. After defeating an opponent for the Player you are Bodyguarding, you gain +25% EXP. 

[*]Adventurer - Your quests take -250 wpd to a minimum of 250. Whenever you complete a quest gain 100z.

[*]Bandit King -  Whenever you kill an opponent in Battle, you may take one non-ship, non-custom item from their inventory and add it to your own. Your opponent's escape rolls gain -2 to the result.

[*]Scientist - You start the game with +5 Inventory Slots. At the start of the game, you may choose 1 Technology item to be added to your Inventory.

[*]Healer - Your recover rate is +10%. Once per week you may interrupt a match on your planet (non tournament) and heal one combatant for (15% you total HP).
[/list][/color]
