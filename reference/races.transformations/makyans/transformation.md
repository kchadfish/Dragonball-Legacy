Makyan Transformations

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 1 — Makyan Starchild[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Pupils vanish and a smoke-like essence blends into the aura.
Stats: +20% Power, +20% HP, +10% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] When Makyo Megastar triggers, opponent attack results -2 until their next successful single-die attack; does not stack.
[INTERMEDIATE] Choose a second Makyo Megastar number; same -2 effect.
[MASTERED] Two numbers; each triggers within ±1 of the chosen values; same -2 effect.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 2 — Super Makyan[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: A stronger Makyo-powered state.
Stats: +40% Power, +40% HP, +20% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Two Makyo numbers, trigger within ±1; opponent -3 attack results until next successful single-die attack.
[INTERMEDIATE] Trigger window expands to within 2 of chosen numbers; -3.
[MASTERED] Choose two additional numbers; within-2 trigger; -3.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 3 — Super Makyan Starchild[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Further amplifies the Starchild connection.
Stats: +60% Power, +60% HP, +30% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Choose two more numbers; trigger within 2; opponent -4.
[INTERMEDIATE] Choose two more numbers; trigger within 3; opponent -4.
[MASTERED] Choose three more numbers; trigger within 3; opponent -4.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 4 — Immortal Makyan[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Peak Makyan state tied most strongly to the Makyo Star.
Stats: +70% Power, +90% HP, +40% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Choose three more numbers; trigger within 3; opponent -5.
[INTERMEDIATE] Choose three more; trigger within 4; opponent -5.
[MASTERED] Choose four more; trigger within 4; opponent -4 (as written in the source).
