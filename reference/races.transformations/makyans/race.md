Makyan

[hr]
Source: Makyans
Biography: Pointed-eared, fanged natives of the roaming Makyo Star. Their combat potential is linked to their homeworld, and they can divert defeated foes into the Dead Zone.

[color=#ee4a2d]RACIAL TRAITS[/color]
[hr]
[color=#ee4a2d]Makyo Megastar[/color] - Before round one, choose a number. When your natural single-die attack result equals it, gain 2 Ki and end opponent-created effects currently affecting you.
[color=#ee4a2d]Dead Zone Vacation[/color] - After killing an opponent in Battle, you may send them to the Dead Zone instead of the Afterlife; they return at the end of the following week to the planet where they died.

[color=#ee4a2d]Classes[/color]
[hr]
[color=#ee4a2d]Guardian of the World[/color] - Choose a planet. There, all stats +10% and quests take -100 WPD to minimum 100. Planet may change each Saga.
[color=#ee4a2d]Head Makyan[/color] - When another character takes your place in Battle through a racial/class effect, both characters gain EXP; +50% EXP if the substitute wins without an escape. That player does not roll interference against your battles.
[color=#ee4a2d]Makyan Minion[/color] - Choose another player each week; on the same planet, you may fight their Battles without an interference roll. Winning for them grants +25% EXP.
[color=#ee4a2d]Dead Zone Gatekeeper[/color] - Dead Zone Vacation does not affect you unless allowed. Twice/Saga revive someone from Dead Zone to a chosen planet. When sending a foe there, offer: surrender up to 2 chosen inventory items and go to Afterlife, or remain in Dead Zone an extra week.
[color=#ee4a2d]Makyo Star Connection[/color] - New transformations start at 1d70; choose a second Makyo Megastar number.
