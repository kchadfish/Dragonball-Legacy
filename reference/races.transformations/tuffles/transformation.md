Tuffle Transformations

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 1 — Zeta Injection[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Nanite injection opens neural pathways while modestly enhancing the body.
Stats: +20% Power, +15% HP, +15% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Immediately after transforming, deactivate one opponent active skill, ignoring normal deactivation requirements.
[INTERMEDIATE] Same; first transformation also lets you activate a CONSTANT base-cost 2 or less by paying its cost.
[MASTERED] First transformation may activate CONSTANT base-cost 2 or less free. RESTRICTEDx1 deactivate an active opponent skill ignoring requirements, no turn used.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 2 — Omega Experiment[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Painful nanite transformation creates a heat-wave aura and greater mental/physical capability.
Stats: +40% Power, +30% HP, +30% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Once/match activate CONSTANT base-cost 3 or less free without using turn. If you have more active skills, opponent defense -1. RESTRICTEDx1 deactivate active skill ignoring requirements.
[INTERMEDIATE] Same as Novice.
[MASTERED] Same, but opponent defense -2 when you have more active skills.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 3 — Andromeda Strain[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Blue veins/lips, yellow eyes and glossy skin as nanites build circuitry.
Stats: +50% Power, +50% Endurance, +50% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Once/match free CONSTANT <=3; if more active skills, opponent defense -2; choose one opponent non-CONSTANT skill to cost +1 Ki; RESTRICTEDx1 deactivate active skill.
[INTERMEDIATE] Defense penalty becomes -3.
[MASTERED] First transformation activates a CONSTANT <=4 free; if more active skills, defense -3; all opponent non-CONSTANT and CONSTANT skills cost +1 Ki; RESTRICTEDx1 deactivation.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 4 — Project Baby[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Peak nanite state: pupil-less eyes, hardened skin and color-drained hair.
Stats: Source prints +50% Power, +50% Endurance, +50% Dexterity, but also says to distribute 50% among the three stats in 10% increments when unlocked.

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] First transformation free CONSTANT <=3; if more active skills, opponent defense -3; opponent non-CONSTANT +1 Ki and CONSTANT +1 Ki; RESTRICTEDx1 deactivation.
[INTERMEDIATE] Defense penalty becomes -4; other costs remain.
[MASTERED] Defense -4; opponent non-CONSTANT skills +2 Ki and CONSTANT +1 Ki; other effects remain.
