Taifuu-jin Transformations

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 1 — Typhoon Form[/color]
[hr]

Stats: +15% Power, +10% HP, +25% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Multi-die attacks may CRITICAL if the first die qualifies; effects referring to 1d critical parameters apply to the first die; multi-die attacks +1 side.

[hr]

Stats: +30% Power, +20% HP, +50% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Same first-die CRITICAL rule; multi-die attack results +1 each.
[INTERMEDIATE] Multi-die results +2 each.
