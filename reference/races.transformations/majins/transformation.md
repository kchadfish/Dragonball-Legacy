Majin Transformations

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 1 — Evil Form[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Body darkens to gray and becomes emaciated; voice tends toward a rougher, more sinister tone.
Stats: +20% Power, +20% HP, +10% Dexterity

Transformation Abilities:
[NOVICE] Successful single-die attack roll 26+ heals 5% Current HP once per turn; attacks +2% Power damage.
[INTERMEDIATE] Same 26+ healing trigger; attacks +3% Power damage.
[MASTERED] Successful attack roll 25+ heals 5% Current HP once per turn; attacks +4% Power damage.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 2 — Infused Form[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Pink color returns, size/musculature rise, and eyes become black with white or red irises.
Stats: +40% Power, +45% HP, +15% Dexterity

Transformation Abilities:
[NOVICE] Successful attack roll 25+ heals 5% Current HP once/turn; attacks +5% Power damage.
[INTERMEDIATE] Threshold 24+; heal 6% Current HP once/turn; +5% Power damage.
[MASTERED] Threshold 24+; heal 7% Current HP once/turn; +5% Power damage.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 3 — Absorption Form[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Absorption alters clothing and some physical features toward the last absorbed opponent.
Stats: +50% Power, +50% HP, +20% Dexterity

Transformation Abilities:
[NOVICE] On successful roll 24+, heal 7% Current HP once/turn; +5% damage. Absorbing a defeated opponent grants one of their equipped items slot-free until another absorption or higher form.
[INTERMEDIATE] Same, but healing can trigger twice/turn.
[MASTERED] Heal 8% Current HP up to twice/turn; same item-copy effect. Absorbed race bonus: Saiyan/Half-Saiyan/Changeling +30% Power; Namekian/Majin +30% Endurance; Android/Bio-Android +30% Dexterity; Human/Other +10% all stats.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 4 — Kid Form[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Body shrinks to child size while staying muscular; black eyes remain; early mastery is associated with volatile behavior.
Stats: +75% Power, +85% HP, +40% Dexterity

Transformation Abilities:
[NOVICE] Successful attack roll 23+ heals 9% Current HP up to twice/turn; attacks +6% Power damage.
[INTERMEDIATE] Roll 22+ heals 10% Current HP; source wording limits it to once per turn with different dice rolls; attacks +8% Power damage.
[MASTERED] Roll 20+ heals 10% Current HP up to twice/turn; attacks +10% Power damage.
