Namekian Transformations

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 1 — Giant Form[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Grows roughly tenfold, near Oozaru scale.
Stats: +20% Power, +30% HP, +0% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Opponent physical attacks -10% damage; Meditative Preparation is lost in form.
[INTERMEDIATE] Physical attacks against you -15%; your attacks +5% Power damage; Meditative Preparation lost.
[MASTERED] Physical attacks against you -15%; your attacks +5% Power damage; Meditative Preparation no longer removed.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 2 — Super Namek[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Muscle size/definition rise substantially.
Stats: +35% Power, +45% HP, +20% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Opponent attacks -10% damage; this prevention cannot be stopped. Power Up gives opponent next attack -1 result.
[INTERMEDIATE] Same, next attack -2 result.
[MASTERED] Same, next attack -3 result.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 3 — Soul Alignment[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Higher enlightened Super Namek state.
Stats: +55% Power, +65% HP, +30% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Opponent attacks -10%; Power Up gives opponent next attack -4 result.
[INTERMEDIATE] Same -10% and -4.
[MASTERED] Opponent attacks -15%; Power Up gives next attack -5 result.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 4 — Planetary Master[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Muscle and skin harden; a calm but immense permanent aura surrounds the Namekian.
Stats: +70% Power, +90% HP, +40% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Opponent attacks -15%; Power Up gives next attack -5 result.
[INTERMEDIATE] -15%; Power Up makes next attack -6 to all dice results if multi-die.
[MASTERED] -15%; Power Up makes next attack -7 to all dice results if multi-die.
