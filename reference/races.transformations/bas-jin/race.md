Skin Color: Light Green
Appearance: Humanoid w/ 4 Eyes (However, the extra set of eyes is not restricted to the head)
Planet of Origin: Basbas
Biography: The four-eyed Bas-jins were not blessed with genetics for combat, and as such were at one point nearly at the point of extinction due to the natural predators of their homeworld. The average Bas-jin is born with above intellect and mental faculties, however, and when one was able to harness the ability of telekinesis (and later chronokinesis), it leveled the playing field for the race. Fighting back enough to rebuild the race behind walled cities, the Bas-jins regrouped, repopulated, and carved a name for themselves as masters of kinesis techniques. While there is the odd misfit, Bas-jins are impressively mentally fit and have some of the best universities in the galaxy. Those born without such intelligence are generally scorned by their culture and society.

RACIAL TRAITS 
[hr]
Kinesis - RESTRICTEDx2. Use after your opponent’s defensive roll against your single-dice attack roll. You may re-roll your attack roll. You cannot CRITICAL with that re-roll. This cannot effect RESTRICTED attacks.

Four Eyes - Your defensive rolls gain +2 sides.

CLASSES
[hr]
Professor of Telekinesis - RESTRICTEDx3. Use when you perform a single dice attack with a base cost of 3 KI Point or more. That attack cannot be BLOCKED and gains "SUCCESSFUL - If your attack roll result is 15 or more, you may de-activate a CONSTANT Skill in play".

Chronoshifter - Once every two weeks you may add a day to the week you're currently in, this day functions as normal in all respects. The following week you only have 6 days available to you for play. Once every two weeks, you may interfere in your own battle without a roll, using your current stats. 

Telekinetic Bomber - If your opponent’s power level is lower than yours then your opponent skips their first turn. If their power level is higher, you may begin play using or activating any skill in your moveset. When your constant skills are de-activated, you regain ki points equal to the skills cost-1.

Professor of Chronokinesis - Kinesis is RESTRICTEDx4 instead and if your re-roll is successful, you may pay 1 ki to STUN.

Clockstopper - Your escape rolls gain +3 to the combined result. RESTRICTEDx1. Use when you perform an attack that does not have a 'STUN' effect. That attack gains +3 dice sides and "SUCCESSFUL - STUN". If you STUN an opponent with this effect, gain 1 KI Point.
