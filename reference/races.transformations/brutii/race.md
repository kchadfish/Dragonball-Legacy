Skin Color: Traditional human skin tones
Appearance: Humanoid, often large-framed
Planet of Origin: Brutus 
Biography: The Brutii live, breed, and die with physical improvement and combat coursing through their bloodstream. Born naturally large and physically impressive, it is said that only a Brutii could give birth to a Brutii -- a saying which meaning is more than just the obvious. Nearly any other race's women would die on the birthing table, and in fact, their infant mortality rate is the second highest in the galaxy. Despite their large frames and muscular builds, Brutii are not all serious, having personalities ranging as widely as just about anyone else. Bodybuilding and a desire to watch and participate in combat are just two traits that nearly every Brutii share and love, yet their goofy comedy sketch shows are some of the most watched in the West Galaxy -- go figure.

RACIAL TRAITS 
[hr]
Brute Force - Whenever you perform a single dice attack, before you roll your attack roll, you may have it lose all SUCCESSFUL effects to have that attack do +(10% Power) Damage.

Big and Bad - Your opponent's blocks cost +2 Ki Points to use against your single dice attacks.

CLASSES
[hr]
All Brawn - Your attacks do +(5% Power) Damage and gain +2 dice sides. You cannot use CONSTANT Skills.

Bodyguard - At the start of each week, you may choose another Player in the game. You are considered their Bodyguard for the week and may fight their Battles in place of them provided you are on the same planet. After defeating an opponent for the Player you are Bodyguarding, you gain +25% EXP.

Hard To Kill - If a non-signature attack would drop you below 0 Health, but the attack roll result was 12 or less, all damage is negated. When your Health drops below 0 for the first time in combat as the result of an opponent's attack, you may pay 2 Ki to set your Health at 1.

Big Punisher - Your Signature Techniques do +(10% Power) Damage and gain +2 dice sides. RESTRICTEDx1. Use when you perform a physical Advanced Attack. You may have that attack gain +5 dice sides. You may exceed dice modification cap rules with this effect.

Gym Rat - When you gain a new Transformation, you start Mastered with a 1d100 Transformation Roll. Your attack roll dice sides and results cannot be decreased by your opponent's effects.
