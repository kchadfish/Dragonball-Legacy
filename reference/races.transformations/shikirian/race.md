[hr]

[color=#ee4a2d]Power Swap[/color] - RESTRICTEDx1 at start of your turn: choose an opponent and swap how many Ki Points above 0 each has. Usable at 50% Total HP or less; does not use the turn.

[hr]

[color=#ee4a2d]Mercenary[/color] - Start with +1 Weapon; once/week after killing in Battle, take one non-custom, non-spaceship item from victim.

[color=#ee4a2d]Eager to Advance[/color] - Must accept challenges. While you have more Ki than any opponent, opponents cannot manipulate your costs/effects. When a transformation effect grants you Ki, choose an opponent to lose the same amount.
