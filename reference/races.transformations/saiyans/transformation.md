Saiyan Transformations

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 1 — Oozaru[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Tail + full-moon/Power Ball produces a giant ape. Early mastery is uncontrolled.
Stats: +35% Power, +25% HP, -10% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Opponent physical attacks -20%. Each turn roll 1d2; on 2, randomly select an Advanced Attack and use it or pass.
[INTERMEDIATE] Physical damage reduction becomes -25%; random-attack rule remains.
[MASTERED] Physical attacks -25%; your attacks +5% Power damage. Worn-item effects are suppressed except the source’s listed armor/weapon exceptions.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 2 — Super Saiyan[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Golden upright hair, green-blue eyes, defined musculature and golden aura.
Stats: +50% Power, +30% HP, +20% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] May add +1 attack result; that roll cannot SEVER; multi-die only first die gains it. Attacks +5% Power damage.
[INTERMEDIATE] Bonus becomes +2 result; may instead enter Ultra Super Saiyan at +65% Power, +45% HP, -10% Dexterity. +5% damage.
[MASTERED] +2 result; +5% damage; Advanced Attacks with base cost 4+ cost -1 Ki, including normally unmodifiable costs. USS option remains.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 3 — Super Saiyan 2[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Longer rigid gold hair, lightning in aura, greater output.
Stats: +70% Power, +45% HP, +35% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Attack roll may gain +1 side and +1 result; cannot SEVER with that roll; multi-die bonus applies to first die. +5% damage.
[INTERMEDIATE] +2 sides and +1 result; multi-die first result receives the stated bonus; +5% damage.
[MASTERED] +2 sides and +2 result; multi-die first die gains the bonus; +5% damage; Advanced base-cost 4+ costs -1 Ki.

[color=#ee4a2d]Level 4 — Super Saiyan 3[/color]
[hr]
Appearance: Extreme golden hair growth and a far more intense aura.
Stats: +90% Power, +60% HP, +50% Dexterity

Special Trait(s):
[NOVICE] Attack roll gains +1 side/result modification; multi-die first die only; attacks +10% Power damage.
[INTERMEDIATE] Up to +2 sides and +1 result; multi-die benefit expands as listed; +10% damage.
[MASTERED] +2 sides and/or +2 result; first two dice receive the listed bonus; +10% damage; attacks with base cost 4+ cost -1 Ki.
