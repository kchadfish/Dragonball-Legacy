## Hyperbolic Time Chamber
Description: The room of legend has long since been held away from mortals by Korin and the Earth’s Guardian. However after dealing with the events on the lower terrace of the tower, you now have gained entry into a room that seems to go on forever. The air is thin and the gravity is heavy, but there’s months worth of food and water. The biggest feature of the chamber is that time goes by immensely faster. Enter the ultimate training ground, do you have what it takes?
Requirements: Sparring Partner (Joint Quest) // Battle for the Tower
Reward(s): 2.0x Base Exp Gain // You may unlock your next transformation 1 Level early.
Time: 7 Days (3 RPs, 4 Spars) // 750 WPD


## How to Kill a Mockingbird
Description: Central Command of District 8 has a thorn in its side, and they want that thorn nuked back into the stone age. That thorn happens to be Icarrus Carr: talking bird, thief, and smuggler of developing technologies. Icarrus has been spotted at a gas station eating ringdings and eyeing the monolithic Valor Corp™ R&D Facility nearby, and Central Command is waiting for him. By this point, however, they suspect a mole is aiding him.
So they've hired you to protect their findings and stop the thief before he can rob them for the third time this year .... or so that you can be the mole for Icarrus and his heist gang.
Requirements: None
Reward(s): 400z // Once this Saga you may have a quest cost -1 Day and 1/2 WPD, before any other reductions.
Alternate Reward(s): 800z ... OR ... One Technology Item worth 1,300z or less.
Time: 3 Days // 700 WPD

Capsule Corps: Going Green!
Description: Ever since their collaboration with Earth, many of the weaker-caste Namekians have taken a renewed interest in engineering. While they may not be the friendliest with Earth anymore, their friendship with Capsule Corps. is alive and well.
With funding and assistance from Capsule Corps., the Namekians have begun to make their own tech teams! Unlike on Earth, however, these engineering teams do not have companies, and instead work for the good of their village. They’re a bit short on staff right now and could use somebody who can handle heavy lifting. They may have er...lost a shipment or two in the sea connecting their islands. Give them a hand and they may just whip something up for you!
Requirements: None
Rewards: 1 Technology item of 1,500z value or less.
Time: 2 Days // 600 WPD
