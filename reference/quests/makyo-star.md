## Controlling the Deadzone
Description: The priests of the Makyo Star have been worshiping for centuries. It turns out, the Dead Zone is a central tenet of their faiths. They have been practicing for eons on how to utilize the Dead Zone and they say they can teach you. I guess it turns out being able to manipulate the Dead Zone isn't purely for Makyans...
Requirements: 3,500 PL
Reward(s): Instant Transmission
Time: 3 Days // 1,000 wpd
## Instant Transmission
Description: The user dematerializes and moves as a mass of light, disappearing from view at an incredible speed. From that point, they can lock onto a Ki signature and materialize near it. This particular version utilizes the deadzone for movement through space instead of the normal method. They create a deadzone portal and connect the deadzone to another point in space, hopping through and appearing on the other side.
Effect: Once per week, you can Instant Transmission yourself to another planet. You cannot use Instant Transmission to teleport you to another planet 2 weeks in a row. In the event that the planet you are is destroyed, you may use Instant Transmission to take you to another planet (regardless if you used it the week before). Instant Transmission is a requirement to learn certain moves.
Requirements: None
Training Days: 2
## Wreckage in the Storm
Description: The Right Eye of the storm holds ruins beyond measure. Rumors have it that one of the ruins used to be Garlic Jr.'s previous residence. If you explore, maybe you will find something worthwhile or a clue to defeating the monster. Of course, the storm isn't the easiest to navigate through and the locals don't seem willing to help you.
Requirements: 1,000 HP (Base + Gear)
Reward(s): Cracked Time Machine
Time: 3 Days // 600 wpd
Cracked Time Machine [500z]
Inventory Slots: 1
Description: Garlic Jr. has lived for a very long time. This is one of the failed experiments he has done in order to try to gain power. While this time machine may not work very well, it may be somewhat useful for you.

Well, that complicates things.

## Judgment Night [FREESTYLE, PHYSICAL, MANIPULATED KI, PUNCH]
Description: The user fills the surrounding miles with a consuming darkness. The user mixes their own Ki with this unnatural night energy and wraps the thick shadows over his hands, striking at their opponent from the shadows. If utilized properly, they can rip the opponent's shadow out momentarily, reducing them from their current form to their true physical state.
Effect: RESTRICTEDx1. Energy attack doing (40% Power) Damage. Your attack roll is 3d30 for this attack. SUCCESSFUL - If two or more dice rolls are SUCCESSFUL, your opponent is reverted to Base Form. Costs 7 KI Points.

## Trek to War
Description: The Makyo Star is home to many people that feel very strongly for their cause. It's difficult not to, with a ruler like Garlic Jr. after all. Some people have taken this to extremes, however. You've heard of a handful of ruffians that have probably had a little too much to drink, the Red Vests (named appropriately for their strange interest in matching apparel). They've started a fight with another handful of scoundrels, who've taken to calling themselves Storm Squaddies.
Good news for the Red Vests, the Storm Squaddies can't hit the broad side of a barn! Bad news, they still find ways to get hurt. Maybe you can step in and try to quiet things down, before too much destruction happens?
Reward(s): Wrist Mounted Laser
Time: 2 Days // 800 wpd
