## Beat the Beat
Description: A crew of humans have arrived on Shi’kir to share culture and technology...Unfortunately, it’s a group of disco and EDM junkies who only brought a boombox and a light show. Much to everyone’s surprise, the Shikirians have taken to praising this visitors for their ingenuity, but nobody seems to know why. Whatever they have to offer such a war-like race is certainly worth investigating.
Requirements: None
Reward: Weaponized Workout CD
Time: 1 Day // 600 WPD
Weaponized Workout CD [800z]
Item Slots: 0
Description: A portable disk filled with only the sickest of beats and the hottest tunes.
Effect: USEx1. Gain +1 Day this week. One use per character.
## Quick Stop Body Swap
Description: Killru the Terrible has walked the Galaxies for thousands of years, both spreading the seeds of dissent and saving the innocent. He’s grown bored with it all, and has picked up a new hobby in life: to live as every race he can find. Killru has yet to live as your race, and so he approaches you with an offer. His current race seems appealing, and he says it’s only a matter of time before you revert back anyways. Dare you accept his offer?
Requirements: None
Reward: Choose a Race. You have that race’s Racials, one of its Classes, and its Transformations for the following week instead of your own. Any permanent changes made from this effect, such as moves learned or items gained, expire along with this race change.
Time: 1 Days // 500 WPD
## To Catch a Predator
Description: District 2 is looking to improve its current defense system above and beyond what District 6 has to offer, in hopes to attract the best their planet has to offer. They’ve sent negotiators to District 1, 3, 5, and 8 seeking trade deals to mass produce superior systems, but each their messengers were killed in action. Intel suspects foul play from District 6, and the militarized Districts do not respect digital negotiations for it shows weakness.
Officials of District 2 need a new messenger to secure the deal and lay a trap for the expected assassin. Are you up to the challenge?
Requirements: None
Reward: 0.75x Base EXP // One Spaceship Weapon of your choice
Time: 3 Days (2 RP, 1 Battle) // 750 WPD

## Army Drills!
Description: The Shikiran army is known across the Galaxy for its training regimens. The secret, the Shikirian leadership claims, is to only have Shikirians. So...That training’s off the table. Unless of course you were able to get special permission, or spy on them to observe their methods. You’d need a training partner, of course. Fighting a non-Shikirian without a fatality is akin to Shikirian cooties.
Requirements: Joint Quest (1 partner)
Reward: You and your quest-ally teach each other one non-custom Freestyle technique that you know, or Styled technique you know that matches the learner’s Style. These techniques can be Advanced Attacks, Skills, or Blocks. For an additional 3 days, you can choose Signature Techniques.
Time: 4 Days or 7 Days // 700 WPD
## Idle Masters
Description: A conference is going on in one of Shi’kir’s many port cities, and it’s got everyone gossiping. Rumors abound that it’s a conference for all the martial arts trainers across North Galaxy, meeting to demonstrate alternative techniques and combat approaches for the ever-studious masters of war that run Shi’Kir. In all the hubbub about such a grandiose event, no one but you even noticed the cloaked Assassin slip into the conference venue.
Requirements: One Mastery
Rewards: Add a Mastery to your Extra Moves List. You must meet the qualifications for that Mastery. You may switch to that Mastery immediately after completing this quest.
Time: 5 Days // 800 WPD
## Supplies and Demands
Description: The recent war between Districts 3 and 4 have been devastating for both sides, especially the one that was nuked into the stone age. With District 4 reduced to a contained nuclear winter, the multitude of mercenary agencies from within are lost to the ages and the people of Shi’kir, as well as outside clients, have an even greater need for the fighters-for-hire that the planet is so well known for.
Recruiting and training these squads will take time and effort, and securing clients is not a guarantee, but there’s opportunity to be had in the post-war Shi’kir. What are you, if not an opportunist?
Requirements: None
Reward: The agency performs one non-saga quest on any non-Afterlife planet for you. You do not have to meet the requirements, as they will meet them for you. This may not be a quest you have already completed, or the counterpart to a quest you have already completed. You receive the reward for that quest and you are considered to have completed that quest for all effects and purposes. This quest has Days and WPD equal to the quest you choose.
You must fight any battles with your set, though you do not die and are restored to your normal HP after the battle.
Time: X Days // X WPD

## War Never Changes
Description: Scientists have been developing a new weapon of war for use by the highest bidder. A Maguma-Jin black ops squad has infiltrated the corporation's security team and needs a mastery in Kiihakai to integrate the weapon's methods into themselves, for reverse engineering. They have the extraction device and the blue prints, but they're sorely lacking in one top shelf, high quality, mint condition (You).
Requirements: None
Reward(s): You may create a new Kiihakai Styled Technique (Mastery, Skill, Advanced Attack, Signature Technique, or Block) that will be permanently added to the Kiihakai Style and taught on Shi'kir. You may learn this Technique from anywhere by spending the required Training Days -1. This Technique must be approved by an Administrator, and should follow the general theme of the Style.
Alternate Reward(s): 650z
Time: 3 Days // 600 WPD
Note: You obtain the Alternate Reward if you are not a Kiihakai user.
