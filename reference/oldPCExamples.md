### History

The boy lay in the hospital bed looking out of the window on a nice sunny day when a doctor walked in with a six-foot-four Majin with a huge smile on his face. The boy didn't look at them when they approached him. He just stared out of the window, his hands on his lap, frowning. Androxus took the doctor into the hall and they talked about his condition.

“So what's wrong with the kid, doc? Can't be that bad for him.”

“Ah yes, we did some tests to find out that he was slowly losing control of his limbs. His legs stopped working this morning...”

Androxus had been visiting children's hospitals all over Earth to help the kids in any way he could, opening big training halls for them to train in the way of the Vanguard style. When the news caught wind of this, it made him an instant celebrity among families. This child had been told by doctors that he was going to slowly lose control of his body as he got older, and when Androxus got wind of it he would not allow that to happen.

The doctor said there was an experimental new treatment using bio-android parts to fix the humanoid condition. Not thinking twice, Androxus wrote a check for over two million Zenni to personally see that the child received it.

They quickly got people on the phone, and the young boy went into the operation within four weeks. Androxus personally waited for the surgery to finish and greeted him with a smile. The boy's life changed forever that day. They were put on every news station for years and traveled the world with Androxus, who taught him the ways of the Vanguard and took him under his wing as a personal student. SkullØmania was about ten when this happened and was twenty now.

Time and training had turned this struggling kid into a student of the Vanguard. It was time for him to go off on his own and show the world the power of the Vanguard, taking on the persona of SkullØmania, the world's most powerful wrestler and defender of justice.

There she was found and raised by a loving middle aged couple, despite the fact that she was not being human. Libbier lived a peaceful life, being cared and raised as a normal human for 6 years. Unfortunately, despite many warnings from her parent, she one day gazed upon the full moon. Nothing was ever the same.

The destruction caused by a rampaging giant ape that night, lead the authorities to come for Libbier. Her parents, fearing for their daughter's life, escaped with her, turning to the dark side of society for safety. However, they began to fear that eventually their luck would run out, and they would either get caught or be unable to escape another rampaging monster.

It was not easy for Libbier at first, but steadily, she began to learn things she never thought would have interested her. Saiyan blood coursed thru her veins indeed as she was able to quickly adapt to the harsh training the old man threw at her. As she learned new things, old ones also began to make sense. Her unnatural tail, what it meant, and the disaster that that simple glimpse at the shining moon caused in her life.

At the age of 18, Libbier had finally learned everything that the old man could teach her, but it was not enough. She could feel it, inside of her, the ever growing power that was ever so hard to contain.
